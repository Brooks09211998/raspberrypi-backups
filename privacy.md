# Privacy Policy — Raspberry Pi Backups

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

## Overview

Raspberry Pi Backups is a personal, non-commercial backup utility that uses rclone and Google Drive to store and retrieve encrypted backups of Raspberry Pi systems and services.

## Information Accessed

The application requests access to Google Drive to upload, list, download, and manage files necessary for backup and restoration.

The application does not independently collect personal information for advertising, analytics, or marketing.

## Data Storage and Security

Backup archives are encrypted locally before being uploaded to Google Drive.

Google OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. Access to Google Drive is managed through Google's OAuth authorization system.

No Google Drive data is intentionally shared with third parties by this application, apart from the services necessary to operate Google Drive and the backup process.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until deleted by the owner or by configured backup retention procedures.

The owner can delete backup files directly from Google Drive and revoke the application's access through Google Account security settings.

## Google User Data

Google user data accessed through this application is used solely for personal backup and restoration operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, or transferred to unrelated third parties.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
